## Findings

1. **Real confidences change the cross-domain picture (fix 1b).** Same zero-shot predictions, class-agnostic mAP with real scores vs the old constant 0.5: CANSURF 0.305 vs 0.079, FloW 0.076 vs 0.012. The previous EoMT FloW / CANSURF numbers understated EoMT by a factor of 4–6. Zero-shot COCO EoMT labels CANSURF cans mostly as "cell phone", "bottle", "book".
2. **Cross-domain transfer (LaRS → box sets): EoMT beats YOLO.** FloW debris-only: EoMT panoptic 0.076 vs YOLOv11s 0.033; CANSURF: 0.075 vs 0.006.
3. **On LaRS boxes, YOLOv11s leads EoMT** (8 objects: 0.176 vs 0.134). **On LaRS PQ, EoMT leads** (0.405 vs 0.346 for YOLOv11s + SAM), but YOLOv11s + SAM has the best object PQ (0.234 vs 0.204).
4. **Box-prompted SAM fails on Static Obstacle** (the shoreline region). EoMT + SAM: Static Obstacle PQ 0.399 vs 0.885 with EoMT's own masks, obstacle IoU 0.43 vs 0.94; YOLOv11s + SAM: Static Obstacle PQ 0.332. This drags both SAM rows' stuff PQ and mIoU down. Water and sky masks are fine (median IoU vs GT 0.97–0.98).
5. **EoMT full fine-tuning beats LoRA on PQ** (0.405 vs 0.379; things 0.204 vs 0.170); box AP and mIoU about equal. LoRA trains 9.1M of 95.9M parameters. Both arms selected the **last** epoch of the 12-epoch budget ([full](sources/epoch_selection_eomt_lars_full.json), [LoRA](sources/epoch_selection_eomt_lars_lora.json)): longer training may still help.
6. **Held-out protocol + scaled annealing** moved EoMT panoptic from 0.395 (ablation 03) to 0.405 PQ.
7. **YOLOv11s vs YOLOv11n in-domain:** FloW 0.401 vs 0.390, CANSURF 0.860 vs 0.857 (YOLOv11n: ablation 08).
8. **Tightened COCO → LaRS mapping** removed 27 land-animal box predictions; scores unchanged to 4 decimals.
9. **Adding Water / Sky to YOLO** (for YOLO + SAM): held-out object AP 0.203 vs 0.215 for the 9-class model ([11-class](sources/fix2_held10_box_full_11s_11cls_stageA_28515723.json), [9-class](sources/fix2_held10_box_full_11s_stageA_28515719.json)), a 0.012 drop (> 0.01, reported); on LaRS val 0.175 vs 0.176.
10. **YOLO + SAM operating point** chosen on held10 with the stage-A model: confidence 0.20 (held10 PQ 0.407; sweep 0.05–0.5 in [the pilot](sources/sam_panoptic_yolo_held10_pilot_28515718.json)).

11. **EoMT trained on SAM masks is capped by label tightness, not detection.** Boxes taken straight from the SAM masks score only 0.461 (FloW) / 0.601 (CANSURF) box AP against the GT boxes, with AP50 ~0.97: the GT boxes are looser than the objects, so mask-derived boxes fail the high-IoU thresholds. At AP50, EoMT matches or beats YOLOv11s on CANSURF (0.967 vs 0.952) and trails on FloW (0.670 vs 0.827); at AP75 it trails on both (0.602 vs 0.863; 0.110 vs 0.333). The mAP@[.50:.95] gap to YOLO (FloW 0.236 vs 0.401, CANSURF 0.631 vs 0.860) therefore mixes model quality with a label-format penalty that YOLO, trained on the GT boxes, does not pay. Report AP50 next to mAP for these rows.
12. **In-domain training dominates transfer for EoMT too:** FloW 0.236 vs 0.137 from LaRS, CANSURF 0.631 vs 0.090.
13. **Fix 4 LaRS checkpoint transfers better than ablation 03's** (FloW debris-only 0.137 vs 0.076, CANSURF 0.090 vs 0.075), consistent with the held-out protocol and scaled annealing.
14. **LoRA transfers worse on debris-only, not on class-agnostic.** LaRS LoRA → CANSURF: debris-only 0.023, class-agnostic 0.161 (best of the cross rows); LaRS LoRA → FloW 0.049 vs 0.090. The LoRA model finds the objects but gives them non-debris LaRS classes. In-domain, LoRA is within 0.016 of full fine-tuning (FloW 0.220 vs 0.236, CANSURF 0.628 vs 0.631) with 9.1M of 95.9M parameters trained.
15. **Epoch budget:** FloW full again selected the last epoch (12); CANSURF full 11, LoRA 9 and FloW LoRA 11 peaked before the end. Stage A curves are flat over the last 3–4 epochs on CANSURF, still rising on FloW.

16. **LoRA was under-trained, not limited by the held-out protocol** (2026-10-26). LaRS LoRA with a 24-epoch budget: held10 PQ 0.404 at lr 1e-4 (epoch 22), **0.452** at lr 5e-4 (epoch 23), vs 0.369 with 12 epochs ([e24 selection](sources/epoch_selection_eomt_lars_lora_e24.json), [e24 lr ×5 selection](sources/epoch_selection_eomt_lars_lora_e24_lr5.json)). Stage B on LaRS val: PQ 0.379 → [0.399](sources/fix1_lars_panoptic_lars_eomt_lora_e24_28592561.json) → [**0.424**](sources/fix1_lars_panoptic_lars_eomt_lora_e24_lr5_28592562.json) (things 0.170 → 0.194 → 0.231; box AP 8 obj 0.132 → 0.144 → 0.152; mIoU unchanged, 97.2–97.3). LoRA at lr ×5 now beats full fine-tuning (0.405), **but full fine-tuning has had only 12 epochs at lr 1e-4**: the comparison is not fair until full fine-tuning gets the same budget and lr check. Both new runs peaked near the end of 24 epochs. Cross-domain (FloW / CANSURF) not yet run for the new checkpoints.
